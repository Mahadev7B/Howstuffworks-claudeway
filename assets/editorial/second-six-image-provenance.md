# Second six image provenance

Created 2026-10-19 with the built-in image-generation tool, using
`transparent_background: false`. No API key, paid image service, AskLilOwl
plugin, or existing site asset was used. All outputs are private editorial
candidates and remain subject to human approval before publication.

The prompt set asked for polished, text-free 16:10 children's science-book
illustrations for ages seven and up, with the named teaching beat as the only
visual story. Every prompt prohibited words, labels, logos, watermarks, borders,
and unrelated fantasy elements. Covers asked for one inviting visual overview;
slide prompts used the following specific scenes:

- Rain: evaporation from a sun-warmed lake; water vapor cooling into tiny cloud
  droplets without visible steam; droplets combining inside a cloud without
  giant cartoon drops; and a complete landscape water cycle.
- Jupiter: an accurate Earth/Jupiter size comparison; colored cloud bands and
  rapid rotation; the Great Red Spot as a long-lived storm; and the four
  Galilean moons around Jupiter.
- Fireflies: light from a firefly abdomen; a restrained cutaway-style chemical
  reaction using oxygen and luciferin; distinct flash patterns in a meadow; and
  cool bioluminescent light contrasted with a warm lamp.
- Platypuses: a platypus in an eastern Australian freshwater habitat; eggs in a
  protected burrow; a mother caring for newly hatched young; and platypus and
  echidna as the living monotreme family.
- Jumping spiders: several realistic habitats; large forward-facing eyes;
  stalking and jumping with a silk safety line; and respectful observation by
  a child without handling the spider.
- Photosynthesis: leaves capturing sunlight; roots taking in water while leaves
  take in carbon dioxide; sugar being made and transported through the plant;
  and plants supporting a food web while releasing oxygen.

The first condensation and growing-raindrop candidates were rejected during
visual review because they suggested visible steam and oversized droplets. The
manifest selects the corrected `v2` files below; rejected candidates were not
kept in the project.
