What the site does

AskLilOwl shows short illustrated lessons with narration. Some lessons are published in the library for anyone to watch. Others are made when a parent asks a question. Lesson text, pictures, and spoken audio are produced with automated tools. They can be wrong or incomplete. They are not school instruction, medical advice, or safety advice. Please use your own judgment before a child copies anything a lesson describes.

Watching and asking

  • Anyone can watch a published lesson without an account and without paying. Opening those lessons does not use up the free questions.
  • To ask a question, a parent or guardian enters their email and agrees to the privacy policy and these terms. There is no password.
  • When email sending is turned on, we may send a one-time code to confirm the address. We don't send marketing.
  • The first {{ free_questions }} different questions on that email are free. Asking one of those again does not use another. The first time that email opens a lesson someone else already asked, it does use one of the {{ free_questions }}.
  • After those free questions, new questions stop. The parent can answer a short waitlist question and can ask us to review a request for 2 more free lessons. We add those only after a person reviews the request.
  • A subscription, when the question box offers it, is $4.99 a month through Stripe and lets that email keep asking new questions. The price charged is the price shown at checkout.

Payments

Stripe handles subscription payments on Stripe's own page. AskLilOwl never sees or stores card numbers. We keep Stripe's customer and subscription reference numbers and whether the subscription is active, as described in the privacy policy.

A subscription renews each month until it is cancelled. A parent can cancel from the "Manage subscription" link when it is shown, or by emailing us. If a payment fails, Stripe may mark the subscription inactive, and new questions can stop until it is active again. If a charge looks wrong, email us and we will look at it with you.

The microphone button uses the browser's own speech recognition. We don't record voices. The browser or device maker may process the audio under its own terms.
