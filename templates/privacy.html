AskLilOwl is a website of illustrated, narrated lessons for curious kids. It is made for children to use with a parent or guardian, never on their own.

What we keep

We keep only the questions children ask. We save each question with the lesson we make for it, so that when another child asks the same question, we can show the same lesson again instead of making a new one.

Please don't let your child type names, schools, addresses or other personal details into a question.

What we don't do

We don't sell information, and we don't show ads.

Changes
